# Old Squarespace news URLs

Recovered from the live riverview-square.com on 2026-09-08, while the old site
was still up. Every old `/news/<slug>` below must redirect to its new path or
an inbound link — a press email, a Google result, a partner's website — breaks.

Five of the old slugs are opaque Squarespace ids rather than readable text, so
there is no way to re-derive this mapping once the old site is switched off.
That is why it is committed here rather than left in scratch.

Task 15 consumes this table to build the redirect config.
